How a referral works

Three steps. Confidential throughout.

1 · Share your numbers

Tell us your skills, bill rate and current split. Takes five minutes. Nothing leaves ClearShare.

2 · Authorize an introduction

We show you a matching employer. We introduce you only after you approve that specific introduction, in writing.

3 · Decide on your terms

You and the employer agree the rate, the transfer and the start date directly. Accept, negotiate or walk away.

Your current employer is never contacted. One authorization covers one employer; it is never treated as blanket permission.

Straight answers

What you are probably asking.

Will my current employer find out?

No. We never contact your current employer, and nothing is shared with anyone until you authorize a specific introduction.

Do I keep my client and project?

Often, yes. Where the client permits, you continue the same project and only the employer behind you changes. Where it does not, the referred employer places you on a new one.

Will there be a gap in my status?

Referred employers file H-1B transfers and OPT paperwork before you move, so you start on the new payroll without a break. Timing is confirmed with the employer's counsel.

What does it cost me?

Nothing. You never pay ClearShare.

Can I change my mind?

Yes, at any point. We make an introduction only when you ask, and the decision to accept an offer is yours alone.

How long does it take?

Five minutes to share your numbers. Introductions typically follow within a few business days.
